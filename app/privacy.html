Privacy Policy (PLACEHOLDER)

⚠️ This is a placeholder and must be completed before any public traffic. It documents intent so the data-collection footprint is explicit during validation.

What we collect

  • Approximate location — when a host taps "Use my location," your device's coordinates are used once to search for nearby restaurants. A manual text fallback is available instead.
  • Anonymous session — a random per-tab ID and a generated nickname (e.g. "Hungry Otter"). No name, email, or account.
  • Swipe activity — your yes/no choices within a room, used to compute matches.
  • Validation analytics — room/participant/swipe/match counts and time-to-match, to learn whether the product works. No advertising identifiers.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, no passwords, no third-party ad tracking.
  • Restaurant data from a paid provider (if enabled) is cached only for the brief lifetime of a room, per that provider's terms — never resold or persisted long-term.
